Exploring how AI can make shopping easier

\n

I spent four years in e-commerce and product development at Macy's, then went to Chicago Booth for my MBA. Last summer I analyzed and improved marketing automation processes at Amazon, which kept me thinking: how do you take the friction out of the shopping experience and make it feel effortless instead? These prototypes are me working through that question, one idea at a time.
